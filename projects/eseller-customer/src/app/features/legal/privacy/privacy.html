1. Information We Collect

Transparency regarding buyer and merchant data

We collect only information necessary to ensure seamless e-commerce transactions, timely parcel deliveries, and platform security:

  • Identity & Contact: Full name, verified email address, mobile phone number for courier dispatch notifications and order verification.
  • Delivery & Shipping Details: Complete street address, city, district/province, and nearest landmark to assist riders in pinpointing your doorstep.
  • Transactional Records: Order items, tracking numbers, invoice amounts, payment methods (Cash on Delivery or Digital Gateway), and return requests.
  • Technical Log Data: IP addresses, browser types, and device signatures used strictly for detecting brute-force attempts and fraudulent checkout activities.

3. Geolocation & Device Location Access

Understanding why we request your location

During registration and order checkout, Eseller may request browser or device GPS location permissions. We use this strictly for:

  • Pinpoint Delivery Accuracy: Verifying local delivery radius and assigning the nearest delivery depot.
  • Fraudulent COD Prevention: Ensuring the order originated from within deliverable geographic boundaries.

Location data is never shared with third-party advertising networks and is stored securely in encrypted server records.

4. Merchant Confidentiality & Commercial Records

Safeguarding partner stores, business KYC and payout credentials

Eseller treats all merchant business details as strictly confidential:

  • Banking & Settlement Data: Merchant IBAN and payout details are stored using AES-256 encryption and are accessible solely for automated disbursements.
  • KYC Documents: National identity cards (CNIC), NTN numbers, and store licenses are reviewed solely by the verification desk and never published publicly.
  • Shopfront Analytics: Your store turnover, visitor analytics, and sales volume are private to your Merchant Admin Portal.
5

5. Security Architecture & Data Safeguards

Enterprise-grade protection across all endpoints

We employ state-of-the-art technical and organizational safeguards:

  • Data in Transit: All web traffic is forced over HTTPS with modern TLS 1.3 encryption.
  • Password Protection: Passwords are never stored in plaintext; they are salted and hashed using cryptographic algorithms.
  • Tokenized Authorization: User sessions leverage secure JSON Web Tokens (JWT) with strict expiration limits and refresh rotations.
  • Strict Role-Based Access: Access to user database records is restricted to authorized platform personnel under confidentiality obligations.

6. Your Privacy Rights & Contacting Us

How to manage or delete your data

You have full ownership of your data on Eseller. You may:

  • Update your profile, phone number, and delivery addresses anytime via your Customer Dashboard.
  • Request an export copy of all order histories and customer logs associated with your email.
  • Request account closure and permanent anonymization of personal records by contacting our Data Protection Officer at privacy@eseller.com.

Questions about your data or privacy settings?

Reach out to our Data Protection Desk anytime at privacy@eseller.com or review our terms.
